About SahayakAI

AI-assisted first-contact support

SahayakAI is a first-contact support prototype designed to help users understand their situation, evaluate safety, review emotional distress, access legal information, and connect with support resources.

It does not diagnose mental illness, determine guilt, or replace official legal or medical processes.
